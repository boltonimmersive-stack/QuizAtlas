A course, not a guessing game.

Each short lesson has three stages:

  1. Understand: read a small, connected set of facts. Study the connection and try the memory exercise.
  2. Guided recall: answer with a first-letter cue available. Compare your answer and decide whether you recalled it.
  3. Check: recall the same facts in a different order without hints. Judge your answer against the explanation.

Checks are self-assessed, not automatically marked. A completed lesson means you have finished its check, not that you have permanently mastered it. Review learned facts when they become due; successful recalls spread out over time.

Old flashcard ratings are retained. Unseen facts stay out of the due-review queue until you study them or choose free practice. Nothing syncs automatically between devices: use a backup to transfer your progress and notes.
